## Method lessons for the next metro
Firecrawl "query" format is unreliable (empty answers); use markdown with onlyMainContent or includeDomains searches by exact event name. CivicPlus sites render "Loading": use search excerpts. Kit needs a stub city html (us_city.py) and `uniq.py` crashes on a single page (uniq2.py). Merge same-host programs up front or Price not listed pages fall under 60% unique. A lead must verify "searched every town" claims: two of the first three helpers built under 15 events each.
